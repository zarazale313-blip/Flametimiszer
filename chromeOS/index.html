Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Chromebook application available?

Not yet. The Chromebook version is planned and will be developed separately.

Not normally. ChromeOS and Windows use different application environments, which is why Flametimiszer will have separate platform support.

Will every Chromebook be supported?

Support will depend on the capabilities of the Chromebook and the ChromeOS environment.
